Quiet hours keep your phone silent for part of the day. Messages below the break-through level arrive without sound, and Beep Me skips text messages. Use this page to set quiet hours and to predict what each channel does.

## Settings

Quiet hours are off by default. To turn them on, open [Routing](/routing). In the **Quiet hours** card, turn on **Use quiet hours**.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start** | 22:00 | The time quiet hours start. |
| **End** | 07:00 | The time quiet hours end. A message at exactly 07:00 is not quiet. |
| **Days** | Every day | The days that quiet hours start on. |
| **These still make sound** | **Urgent only** | The break-through level. Select **High and urgent** to let High through too. |

Select **Save quiet hours** after you change **Start**, **End**, **Days**, or **These still make sound**. The **Quiet now** badge shows on the card when the current time is inside quiet hours.

When **Start** and **End** are the same time, quiet hours do not start.

## Time zone

Beep Me reads **Start** and **End** in your time zone. When you first sign in, Beep Me takes the time zone from your browser. To change it, open [Settings](/settings). Then select a zone under **Time zone**.

## Overnight windows and days

When **Start** is later than **End**, the window runs overnight. The hours after midnight belong to the day the window started.

For example, set **Start** to 22:00, **End** to 07:00, and **Days** to Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, and Fri:

| Time | Quiet | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friday 23:00 | Yes | The window starts on Friday. |
| Saturday 03:00 | Yes | This part belongs to the Friday window. |
| Saturday 23:00 | No | Saturday is not selected. |
| Monday 03:00 | No | This part belongs to the Sunday window. |
| Monday 23:00 | Yes | The window starts on Monday. |

## Break-through

A message at or above the break-through level is not quiet. It goes to its route with sound, text channels included.

Beep Me checks the priority after it applies the agent's highest priority. An agent with a highest priority of High cannot break through when the level is **Urgent only**. See [Priorities](/docs/routing/priorities).

## What each channel does

Beep Me marks each quiet message for silent delivery where the channel supports it.

| Channel | During quiet hours |
| --- | --- |
| Twilio SMS, Telnyx SMS | Skipped |
| Telegram | Sent with notifications turned off for the message |
| Discord | Sent with the suppress notifications flag |
| ntfy | Sent at ntfy priority 1 |
| Pushover | Sent at Pushover priority -1. Low and Medium are sent at -2. |
| Slack, Gmail, Resend, SMTP, webhook | Sent as usual. These channels have no silent option. |

The table on the [channels overview](/docs/channels) shows the same silent support in its last column. Each channel page tells what the provider does with these values.

## When Beep Me skips a text

Beep Me removes text channels from a quiet message. When no channel is left, Beep Me uses the [fallbacks](/docs/routing/routes-and-rules#fallbacks). It tries the lower routes, then the first channel that is not a text channel.

When a quiet message has no channel left that is not a text channel, Beep Me holds it. The status is **Muted**, and Beep Me does not send it later.

## Routing notes

The delivery log adds one of these notes to each quiet message:

```text
Quiet hours: delivered without sound.
Quiet hours: skipped the text. Delivered without sound.
Quiet hours: skipped the text and sent to Telegram without sound.
Quiet hours: Beep Me held the text. No other channel can take this message, so Beep Me sent nothing.
```

When only some of the channels have a silent option, the note ends with "without sound where the channel allows it". When none of them has one, the note ends with "These channels have no silent option".

A quiet message also shows a **Quiet** badge in [Activity](/). Its details show **Quiet hours: Yes**, or **Quiet hours: Held** when Beep Me held the message.
